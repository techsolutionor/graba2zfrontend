Going on a first date is stressful. We want to look, feel, and smell our best. I am here to help you with the “smell” part. The scent we choose for a first date carries many nuances, some of which we’re not even aware of. Sure, we want to smell good. But if you were to ask me, I’d tell you that it goes far beyond that. You can pick up any good-smelling fragrance and call it a day, but this is the fragrance that will be associated with this forever, regardless of how the date goes. Imagine you end up marrying this person, and your scent was subpar…
s No, you want a scent that is unforgettable. One filled with sensuality and will make your date want to get closer to you involuntarily. This is the first impression you’re making on this person in the realm of romance. So, whether you’re meeting them for the first time or they’re an old friend you finally got the guts to ask out, you want a perfume so good that they’ll always think of this day whenever because of it.
Here are some fragrances that I think of to this day, due to how remarkable and irresistible I have found them. I believe they’ll be excellent first date fragrances.

YSL Libre Le Parfum is perfect for a first date. I’m going to be honest, when I first tried the EDP version of Libre, I didn’t think it was anything extraordinary. But the le parfum version is something else. The way this scent is memorable is impressive. The sweetness from the vanilla, honey, and tonka bean gives it a sophisticated gourmand flavour that is both sexy and comforting.
