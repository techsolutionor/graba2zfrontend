Cookies Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Grabatoz! Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and ensure the smooth functioning of our services. By continuing to use our site, you agree to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help us recognize your device and store information about your preferences or past actions.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • 1. Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function correctly. Enable core features such as security, network management, and accessibility.
  • 2. Performance Cookies: Collect data about site usage to improve functionality and user experience.
  • 3. Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences, such as language and login information.
  • 4. Targeting Cookies: Track your browsing behavior to deliver personalized ads and measure ad effectiveness.

How We Use Cookies

  • To Provide Essential Services: Ensure secure login and fraud prevention.
  • To Improve User Experience: Analyze website usage to enhance performance and usability.
  • To Personalize Content: Store preferences for a customized experience.
  • To Deliver Targeted Ads: Show relevant ads based on your browsing behavior.

Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings. Disabling them may affect site functionality.

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers let you control cookies in their settings. Refer to your browser’s help section for details.
  • Opting Out of Targeted Advertising: You can opt out using industry tools like the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) or the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI).

Third-Party Cookies

We may use cookies from trusted third parties for advertising and analytics. These parties have their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Please revisit this page to stay informed about our practices.
